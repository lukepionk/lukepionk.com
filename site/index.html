Program leadership

Why I changed the approach

The first version asked a model to handle the whole review. Its answers varied between runs.

I moved explicit requirements into repeatable checks, used AI for interpretation, and kept final decisions with reviewers.

I use agent evaluations and user feedback to check both behavior and usefulness.
