01 / How I work

The work is technical. The approach is human.

Difficult problems rarely stay inside one discipline. These are the principles I carry across them.

01

Start with the work.

Understand the process, incentives, and people before choosing a tool.

02

Earn trust.

Make the system clear enough to inspect and reliable enough to use when it matters.

03

Use the whole range.

Business judgment, technical depth, and operating context belong in the same room.

04

Stay accountable.

Build, measure, operate, and improve the result—not just the first version.
