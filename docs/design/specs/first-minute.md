# A clear first minute

Status: local draft, October 6, 2026. Updates the opening and content hierarchy
in [v3](v3.md); the visual style and five-section structure remain.

## Purpose

Treat a visit of 60 seconds or less as a design constraint, not an observed
traffic statistic. Visitors should understand what Luke builds, what it enables
other people to do, and what he is exploring outside work.

## Decisions

- Lead with engineering and enablement together: systems teams can build on,
  and help putting them to work.
- Keep three short examples visible in the introduction: a shared data
  platform, an AI review agent, and a personal school-navigation prototype.
  No tour, playback, or choice is required to read them.
- Describe the data platform as a foundation for colleagues' reports and
  dashboards. Separate Luke's ownership of the platform from their outputs.
- Show cross-team enablement through work helping others apply the agent's
  findings. Do not invent training sessions, testimonials, or adoption metrics.
- Keep pilot scope and prototype status in the opening. Do not imply the
  school project already has family users.
- Lead the personal project with the parent's problem. Explain its operation
  plainly; put implementation and evaluation details in a native disclosure.
- Keep career background, credentials, and learning resources available after
  the opening examples. Preserve the existing contact paths.
- Keep employer system names, team identities, team sizes, and operating data
  out of public copy.
- Retain static HTML and CSS. An optional animation is deferred until the
  readable story has been tested with people.

## Validation

Browser checks cover small screens, keyboard interaction, accessibility,
no-JavaScript reading, disclosures, and asset fingerprints.

A human comprehension check remains outstanding: show the page to five people
unfamiliar with Luke's work for 60 seconds, then ask what he builds, what others
can do because of it, and what he is building outside work. Technical checks
cannot establish that visitors retain these takeaways.
